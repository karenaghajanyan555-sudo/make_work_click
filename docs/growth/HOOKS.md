# Hook library and method

A hook is three things that must agree: the **opening visual** (frame 0–1 s), the **written hook**
(on-screen words) and the **spoken hook**. The visual comes first (PLAYBOOK.md § 3). Every hook
must be fully paid off by the video. None of these hooks has audience data behind it yet: they
are candidates, not proven winners (LEARNINGS.md records what actually worked).

Flags: **[proof]** needs a number we measured ourselves or a primary source before use ·
**[verify]** a product capability to confirm on the vendor's docs today · **[tool]** names or
shows a real product — needs the owner's real-tool decision (PLAYBOOK.md § 8b) · no flag = can be
made with today's rules (fictional, illustrative UI).

## Method — for every video
1. Write **10 hooks** from at least **5 categories** below (adapt, never copy a line twice).
2. Score each 0–2 on five criteria (max 10):
   - **Context first**: who it is for / what it is about in the first 3–4 words.
   - **Gap**: leaves a question the viewer wants answered.
   - **Specific**: a concrete noun, number, moment or setting — not a theme.
   - **Stakes**: what it costs them (time, money, a client, an evening).
   - **Visual**: frame 0 can show it, big and legible, without the words.
   **Truth gate** (pass/fail, before scoring): the video pays it off completely; every number is
   sourced or measured; no exaggeration. Fail = discard.
3. Keep the **top three**, write the opening visual for each, choose one, and write two sentences
   on why it should work (mechanism + who it stops). Record all ten and the scores in the
   storyboard (`docs/storyboards/<id>.md`) so LEARNINGS.md can compare later.
4. Variety: the chosen hook's category and sentence pattern differ from the last three videos.

## Retired for now (overused in v001–v009)
"X shouldn't depend on Y" (v001, v003) · "X shouldn't take Y" (v008) · "Every X …" (v002, v004,
v009) · "… runs / sends / writes itself (themselves)" · "Let the system …". Banned anyway (STORY.md):
"Are you tired of", "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge", "leverage", fake statistics.
